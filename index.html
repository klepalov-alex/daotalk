Three lesson types designed for different learning goals and budgets.

1-on-1 Lessons
● 1 student · private

Total focus on you. The teacher adapts every minute of the lesson to your pace, goals, and weak spots.

  • Fully personalised lesson plan
  • Fastest progress — no waiting for others
  • Direct feedback on pronunciation and tones
  • Reschedule anytime with 12 h notice

👥
Small Group
● 2–3 students · semi-private

The sweet spot between personal attention and peer motivation. Practice with a study buddy at a lower cost.

  • Peer conversation practice built in
  • Teacher still knows every student's level
  • More affordable than private lessons
  • Great for couples or friends learning together

Group Lessons
● up to 5 students · group

A community classroom feel, online. Best for structured HSK-prep courses and themed cultural sessions.

  • Fixed weekly schedule — great for routine
  • Most affordable option per session
  • Diverse discussion with multiple learners
  • Ideal for beginners building confidence

Why Learn Chinese?

One in five people on Earth speaks Mandarin. Here's why you should too.

Mandarin is the language of the world's largest economy — and the most spoken language on Earth.

Chinese isn't just another language on your CV. It's a gateway to 5,000 years of philosophy, literature, and art — and to the fastest-growing business network in the world. Whether your goal is career advancement, travel, cultural curiosity, or connecting with family roots, Mandarin opens doors that no other language can.

  • 🌏Over 1.4 billion native speakers — the most spoken mother tongue globally
  • 💼China is the world's largest trading partner for over 120 countries
  • 🎓HSK certification is recognised by universities and employers worldwide
  • 🧠Learning characters strengthens memory, focus, and cognitive flexibility
  • 🎭Unlock film, music, literature, and humour that never survives translation
